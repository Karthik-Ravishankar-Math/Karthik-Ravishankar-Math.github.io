Computability theory & mathematical logic

I’m a fifth-year mathematics PhD student at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, working with Joseph Miller in computability theory.

I’m interested in local and global degree-theoretic questions in the Turing and enumeration degrees. I’m currently working on degree spectra in computable structure theory and join irreducibility in the local structure of the enumeration degrees.

I also work in Lean and am interested in formalizing mathematics.

In my work on Ahmad pairs, I showed that the left half must be low3 and the right half high2.
