Contrasting the Halves of an Ahmad Pair

Karthik Ravishankar

We study Ahmad pairs in the Σ02 enumeration degrees. The left halves are precisely the degrees that are low3 and join irreducible. We prove that every right half is high2. We also characterize the left halves of Ahmad n-pairs using a hierarchy of join irreducibility notions. These results have consequences for the ∀∃ theory of the local structure of the enumeration degrees.
