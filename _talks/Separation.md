Incomparable Σ02 sets (A,B) form an Ahmad pair if every set strictly enumeration below A is also enumeration below B. 
Extending previous work, in this talk we will characterize the left halves of Ahmad pairs as precisely the low3 and 
join irreducible sets and show that the right halves cannot be low3 thereby giving a natural separation between the two halves.
