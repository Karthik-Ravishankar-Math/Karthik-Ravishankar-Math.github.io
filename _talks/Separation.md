Incomparable <math aria-label="Sigma superscript 0 subscript 2"><msubsup><mi mathvariant="normal">Σ</mi><mn>2</mn><mn>0</mn></msubsup></math> sets <math aria-label="pair A, B"><mo>(</mo><mi>A</mi><mo>,</mo><mi>B</mi><mo>)</mo></math> form an Ahmad pair if every set strictly enumeration below <math aria-label="A"><mi>A</mi></math> is also enumeration below <math aria-label="B"><mi>B</mi></math>.
Extending previous work, in this talk we will characterize the left halves of Ahmad pairs as precisely the <math aria-label="low 3"><msub><mtext>low</mtext><mn>3</mn></msub></math> and
join irreducible sets and show that the right halves cannot be <math aria-label="low 3"><msub><mtext>low</mtext><mn>3</mn></msub></math> thereby giving a natural separation between the two halves.
