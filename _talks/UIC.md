The local structure of the e-degrees refers to the e-degrees below <math aria-label="zero prime"><msup><mn>0</mn><mo>′</mo></msup></math>. These correspond precisely to the degrees of the <math aria-label="Sigma 2"><msub><mi mathvariant="normal">Σ</mi><mn>2</mn></msub></math> sets. We shall talk about enumeration reducibility, its connection to Turing reducibility as well as contrast the local structure of the e-degrees with that of the Turing degrees. After a survey of known results we shall end with our new work and some open questions.
