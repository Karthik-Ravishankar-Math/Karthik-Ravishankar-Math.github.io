 A pair <math aria-label="pair A, B"><mo>(</mo><mi>A</mi><mo>,</mo><mi>B</mi><mo>)</mo></math> of <math aria-label="Sigma 2"><msub><mi mathvariant="normal">Σ</mi><mn>2</mn></msub></math>-sets forms an Ahmad pair if every set strictly below <math aria-label="A"><mi>A</mi></math> is also below <math aria-label="B"><mi>B</mi></math>. We give a characterization for when a set can be the left half of an Ahmad pair.
 We also introduce a hierarchy of Ahmad <math aria-label="n"><mi>n</mi></math>-pairs in the local structure and characterize them using different notions of join irreducibility.
 The eventual goal is to come up with a property separating the two halves of an Ahmad pair.
