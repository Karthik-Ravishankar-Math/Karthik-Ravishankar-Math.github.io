 A pair (A,B) of Σ2-sets forms an Ahmad pair if every set strictly below A is also below B. We give a characterization for when a set can be the left half of an Ahmad pair.
 We also introduce a hierarchy of Ahmad n-pairs in the local structure and characterize them using different notions of join irreducibility.
 The eventual goal is to come up with a property separating the two halves of an Ahmad pair. This is joint work with Joe Miller and Mariya Soskova
