 We study Ahmad pairs in the <math aria-label="Sigma superscript 0 subscript 2"><msubsup><mi mathvariant="normal">Σ</mi><mn>2</mn><mn>0</mn></msubsup></math> enumeration degrees. We say <math aria-label="pair A, B"><mo>(</mo><mi>A</mi><mo>,</mo><mi>B</mi><mo>)</mo></math> form an Ahmad pair if <math aria-label="A is not enumeration reducible to B"><mi>A</mi><msub><mo>≰</mo><mi>e</mi></msub><mi>B</mi></math> and every <math aria-label="Z is strictly enumeration reducible to A"><mi>Z</mi><msub><mo>&lt;</mo><mi>e</mi></msub><mi>A</mi></math> satisfies <math aria-label="Z is enumeration reducible to B"><mi>Z</mi><msub><mo>≤</mo><mi>e</mi></msub><mi>B</mi></math>. Ahmad pairs have recently drawn interest as they are a key obstacle in solving the <math aria-label="for all, there exists"><mo>∀</mo><mo>∃</mo></math> theory of the local structure.

In this talk we characterize the left halves of an Ahmad pair as precisely the <math aria-label="low 3"><msub><mtext>low</mtext><mn>3</mn></msub></math> and join irreducible degrees. We also show that right halves cannot be <math aria-label="low 3"><msub><mtext>low</mtext><mn>3</mn></msub></math> . This is a natural separation between the two halves and is a significant strengthening of previous work.

We then define a hierarchy of join irreducibility notions using which we characterize the left halves of Ahmad <math aria-label="n"><mi>n</mi></math>-pairs as those that are <math aria-label="low 3"><msub><mtext>low</mtext><mn>3</mn></msub></math> and <math aria-label="n"><mi>n</mi></math>-join irreducible. This allows us to extend and clarify previous work to show that for any <math aria-label="n"><mi>n</mi></math> there is a set <math aria-label="A"><mi>A</mi></math> which is the left half of an Ahmad <math aria-label="n"><mi>n</mi></math>-pair but not of an Ahmad <math aria-label="n plus 1"><mo>(</mo><mi>n</mi><mo>+</mo><mn>1</mn><mo>)</mo></math>-pair.
