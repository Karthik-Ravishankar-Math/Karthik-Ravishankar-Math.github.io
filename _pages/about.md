I'm a 4th year PhD student working with my advisor, Joseph Miller in Computability theory, a subfield of Mathematical logic. I'm broadly interested in local and global degree theoritic questions in the Turing and the Enumerations degrees.  I'm currently working on a project in computable structure theory related to degree spectra. A past project involved Ahmad pairs in the local structure of the e-degrees, where I was able to show that the left half has to be low_3 and the right half high_2. Related to this, I'm currently thinking about join irreducibility in the local structure of the enumeration degrees.

Before my time at Madison, I completed a B.Tech in Computer Science and Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Technology-Hyderabad. I am originally from the beautiful city of Bengaluru and have lived there most of my life. I enjoy reading, cooking, powerlifting, running and outdoor time with my Bernese Mountain Dog Baloo.
