We study Ahmad pairs in the <math aria-label="Sigma 2"><msub><mi mathvariant="normal">Σ</mi><mn>2</mn></msub></math> enumeration degrees.
<math aria-label="pair A, B"><mo>(</mo><mi>A</mi><mo>,</mo><mi>B</mi><mo>)</mo></math> is an Ahmad pair if <math aria-label="A is not enumeration reducible to B"><mi>A</mi><msub><mo>≰</mo><mi>e</mi></msub><mi>B</mi></math> and every <math aria-label="Z is strictly enumeration reducible to A"><mi>Z</mi><msub><mo>&lt;</mo><mi>e</mi></msub><mi>A</mi></math> satisfies <math aria-label="Z is enumeration reducible to B"><mi>Z</mi><msub><mo>≤</mo><mi>e</mi></msub><mi>B</mi></math>.
We characterize the degrees that are the left halves of an Ahmad pair
as those that are <math aria-label="low 3"><msub><mtext>low</mtext><mn>3</mn></msub></math> and join irreducible. We then show that the right
half has to be <math aria-label="high 2"><msub><mtext>high</mtext><mn>2</mn></msub></math> giving a natural separation between the two halves
which is a significant strengthening of previous work. We define a hier-
archy of join irreducibility notions using which we characterize the left
halves of Ahmad <math aria-label="n"><mi>n</mi></math>-pairs as those that are <math aria-label="low 3"><msub><mtext>low</mtext><mn>3</mn></msub></math> and <math aria-label="n"><mi>n</mi></math>-join irreducible,
while the right halves are <math aria-label="high 2"><msub><mtext>high</mtext><mn>2</mn></msub></math>. This allows us to extend and clarify
previous work to show that for any <math aria-label="n"><mi>n</mi></math>, there is a set <math aria-label="A"><mi>A</mi></math> which is the left
half of an Ahmad <math aria-label="n"><mi>n</mi></math>-pair but not of an Ahmad <math aria-label="n plus 1"><mo>(</mo><mi>n</mi><mo>+</mo><mn>1</mn><mo>)</mo></math>-pair. These re-
sults have new implications about the <math aria-label="for all, there exists"><mo>∀</mo><mo>∃</mo></math>-theory of the <math aria-label="Sigma 2"><msub><mi mathvariant="normal">Σ</mi><mn>2</mn></msub></math> e-degrees as
a partial order and also provide a new <math aria-label="Pi 3"><msub><mi mathvariant="normal">Π</mi><mn>3</mn></msub></math> definition of <math aria-label="low 3"><msub><mtext>low</mtext><mn>3</mn></msub></math> as well as
<math aria-label="high 2"><msub><mtext>high</mtext><mn>2</mn></msub></math>.
