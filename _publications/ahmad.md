We study Ahmad pairs in the Σ_2 enumeration degrees.
(A, B) is an Ahmad pair if A ̸≤e B and every Z <e A satisfies Z ≤e B.
We characterize the degrees that are the left halves of an Ahmad pair
as those that are low3 and join irreducible. We then show that the right
half has to be high2 giving a natural separation between the two halves
which is a significant strengthening of previous work. We define a hier-
archy of join irreducibility notions using which we characterize the left
halves of Ahmad n-pairs as those that are low3 and n-join irreducible,
while the right halves are high2. This allows us to extend and clarify
previous work to show that for any n, there is a set A which is the left
half of an Ahmad n-pair but not of an Ahmad (n + 1)-pair. These re-
sults have new implications about the ∀∃-theory of the Σ_2 e-degrees as
a partial order and also provide a new Π_3 definition of low3 as well as
high2.
